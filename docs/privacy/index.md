## tiles.versatiles.org and geocode.versatiles.org

When your browser or app loads map tiles, styles, fonts or search results,
the server receives your IP address, the requested address, the referring
website and the User-Agent, as with every web request. I store two kinds
of logs:

- **Request logs**, to find errors, misuse and attacks: time, anonymised
  IP address, path without query string, status, size, response time,
  the referring website (only its address, such as `https://example.com`)
  and the User-Agent. Of an IPv4 address only the first 24 of 32 bits are
  kept, of an IPv6 address only the first 32 of 128 bits. The geocoder
  never stores the search text. tiles.versatiles.org logs only failed
  requests and redirects; geocode.versatiles.org logs every request. The
  servers' own error logs are anonymised the same way. All of these are
  deleted after 28 days.
- **Usage statistics**, to see which websites and apps use the services
  and how much: the referring website (only its address), the requested
  path, size, status and the hour; for tiles.versatiles.org also whether
  the request came through a CDN. They contain no IP address and no
  User-Agent, and are kept without time limit.

The full IP address is used only while the request is answered, and to
limit the number of requests per client (rate limiting). It is not stored.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running the services securely,
finding misuse and knowing who relies on them (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

tiles.versatiles.org runs on my own server, for which the AS250.net
Foundation provides only rack space and the network connection. geocode.versatiles.org is hosted by
Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, who
process the data on my behalf.

## versatiles.org and docs.versatiles.org

These websites are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub
receives your IP address and other request data when you visit them, and
may log it for security purposes; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
I don't receive these logs. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework.

Maps on these websites load data from tiles.versatiles.org and
geocode.versatiles.org, as described above.

## download.versatiles.org

The downloads are served by Cloudflare, Inc., USA, which receives your IP
address and request data; see the
[Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

When you download a file or open the download page, a program run on
Cloudflare's servers on my behalf answers the request. Cloudflare keeps a
log of each request for 7 days, which I can read: time, full IP address,
the approximate location derived from it (country, city, postal code),
network provider, requested address, referring website and User-Agent. I
use it to find errors and misuse and to see how the downloads are used.
Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running the service securely
(Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## Donations

You can donate via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/versatiles-org),
[Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/versatiles) or Stripe (the
donate buttons on this website lead to my own Stripe account). These
services process your payment and store your data under their own privacy
policies. In my account with them I can see your name, the amount and,
depending on the service, your email address. I don't keep any records of
my own. Your name or logo appears on [versatiles.org/sponsors](/sponsors/)
only if you chose to make your sponsorship public.

Legal basis: carrying out your donation (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, and to have it corrected, deleted
or restricted (Art. 15–18 GDPR), to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), and
to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).
Write to [mail@versatiles.org](mailto:mail@versatiles.org).

Anonymised logs can't be matched to you, so I usually can't find your
entries in them.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one
responsible for me: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und
Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin.
